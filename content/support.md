Need a hand, found a bug, or have an idea? Here's how to reach it.

## Report a bug or request a feature

Photo Diary Companion is developed in the open. The best place for bugs and
requests is the issue tracker:

- [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/vlumi/photo-diary-ios/issues)

Please say which iPhone and iOS version, which version of Photo Diary your
server runs, and what you did. Email works too: <ville@misaki.fi>.

## Common questions

**Do I need my own server?**
To see your own photos, yes: the app is a companion for
[Photo Diary](https://github.com/vlumi/photo-diary), version 1.0.7 or later,
and you need an account on it. The built-in demo on the front page works
without one, so you can look around first.

**How do I add my server?**
On your Photo Diary site, sign in, open the user menu and choose *Pair a
device*. Scan the QR code it shows with the app (*+* on the front page, then
*Scan pairing QR code*), or open the link on the iPhone, or copy it and paste
it into the app. A code works once and for two minutes; *New code* makes
another.

**The app says my pairing has expired.**
Sessions last a while but not forever, and removing the app's session on the
site ends it. Pair the device again the same way; the app stays where you were.

**Where are my todo pins?**
On the phone, and nowhere else: not on your server, not in any cloud. They go
with the phone's own backup if you have that on. Deleting the app deletes them.

**Why are some photos missing from the map?**
Only photos with a location are on the map. The calendar shows them all.

**I changed the language and nothing happened.**
The language takes effect the next time you open the app. Quit and reopen it.

**Is there any tracking or ads?**
No. No accounts of ours, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. See the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
