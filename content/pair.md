This link pairs **Photo Diary Companion** with a Photo Diary server. On an
iPhone with the app installed, it opens the app directly. Seeing this page
means the app isn't on this device, or the link was opened somewhere other
than an iPhone.

1. Install Photo Diary Companion on your iPhone{{< appstore-link >}}.
2. On your Photo Diary site, open *Pair a device* again and tap *Open in app*
   on the iPhone, or scan its QR code with the iPhone's camera. A code works
   once and for two minutes; *New code* makes another.

*The pairing code in this link never reached this site: it sits after the `#`
in the address, which the browser keeps to itself.*
